---
name: office-tracking-week-boundaries
description: Office tracking splits weeks at month boundaries; each day belongs to its calendar month, not to the month containing the Monday
type: feedback
---

Office tracking weeks split at month boundaries. Each calendar day belongs to the month it falls in, regardless of when the week started. A week that crosses a month boundary is displayed as two stubs: the tail in the ending month (e.g., "Week 5 (3/30–3/31)") and a continuation in the next month (e.g., "Week 5 cont'd (4/1–4/3)").

**Why:** The old "Monday's month owns the whole week" rule caused absurd outcomes — e.g., March compliance requiring days in April because Week 5's Monday happened to be 3/30. A month's compliance should only depend on calendar days within that month.

**How to apply:** When calculating monthly targets, count only the working days within that calendar month. For a partial week at month-end, prorate the target: 1 available day → 1 needed, 2 days → 1 needed, 3 days → 2 needed, 4 days → 2 needed, 5 days → 3 needed. The continuation stub in the next month follows the same proration. OOO/PTO entries belong in whatever month their date falls in.
