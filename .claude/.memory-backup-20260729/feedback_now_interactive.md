Work OS commands (especially /now) should be interactive and collaborative, not a wall of text. The goal is to help work through items, not just report them.

**Why:** Dumping a ranked list leaves the user to figure out what to do next. That's the wrong model for a task management system.

**How to apply:**
- Present one item at a time, then wait for a response
- Offer concrete actions at each step (draft, resolve, skip, quit)
- Don't summarize everything upfront before asking what to do
- Brief header showing counts is fine; details come one item at a time
- The session should feel like working through a queue together, not reading a status report
