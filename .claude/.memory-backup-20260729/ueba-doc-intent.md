The UEBA Engineering Landscape document (`notes/2026-03-11 - UEBA Engineering Landscape.md`) is a high-level architectural engineering document meant to bring shape to an ambiguous problem space. Its purpose is to lay out tracks of work and make ambiguity visible, not resolve it.

**Why:** The problem space is too broad for upfront answers. Open questions should become action items (RFCs, research, experiments), not blockers.

**How to apply:** When editing, every section should leave the reader knowing what needs to happen next, even if the next step is "investigate and propose." If an open question hangs without a concrete next action, flag it. Don't try to resolve ambiguity in the document itself; convert it to an actionable next step.

The document body (everything above Document Status) will be published to Confluence. Do not use Obsidian wiki-links (`[[Name]]`) in the body; use plain text names instead. The Document Status section stays in Obsidian only and can use wiki-links.
