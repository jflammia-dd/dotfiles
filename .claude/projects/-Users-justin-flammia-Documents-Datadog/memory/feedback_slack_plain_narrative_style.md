For Slack messages, write plain flowing narrative. Do not scaffold with bold section-header labels like "Where we stand.", "The open tension.", "What's still unclear." The narrative should convey status and tension implicitly through the prose, not by announcing it with headers.

**Why:** Justin finds the labeled structure heavy and dislikes explicitly stating "here is the situation" then stating it. Peers read the information from the sentences directly.

**How to apply:** Draft Slack updates as a few short paragraphs. State the facts and the open question as narrative. Save labeled/numbered structure for long-form docs and broadcast announcements, not routine team thread updates. Still apply the rest of [[justins-voice]] (no Oxford commas, no em dashes, active voice). Related: [[feedback_now_interactive]].
