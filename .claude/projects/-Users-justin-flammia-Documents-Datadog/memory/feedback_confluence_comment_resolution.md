Do not suggest resolving Confluence inline comments after posting a reply.

**Why:** Justin leaves threads open so other readers can follow the full discussion. Resolving removes that visibility.

**How to apply:** After posting a reply to a Confluence inline comment, do not mention resolving it or offer to resolve it. The thread stays open by design.
