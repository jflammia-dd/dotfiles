name: Leadership documents define their own terminology and don't borrow external nomenclature
description: Documents written for alignment and awareness should define their own terms rather than referencing phase/wave/cohort labels from external plans.

When writing leadership-facing documents (delivery plans, overviews, alignment docs), define the document's own terminology. Do not borrow labels like "Phase 1", "Wave 1", "Wave 2" from external plans or rollout diagrams unless those terms are defined within the document itself.

**Why:** Terms like "Phase 1" and "Wave 1" carry meaning only if the reader knows what those phases and waves contain. A document that borrows them without defining them forces the reader to hold external context in mind. The document should be the authority, not a derivative.

**How to apply:** If a term originates outside the document (from a PM's rollout diagram, an engineering phase plan, etc.), either define it explicitly in the document or replace it with a term the document itself introduces. Relative terms like "Now / Next / Following" or capability numbers ("Build 1") defined within the document are fine. Labels borrowed from external plans are not.
